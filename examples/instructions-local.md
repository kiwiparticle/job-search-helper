# Job Search Agent Instructions (condensed)

## Goal
A remote or hybrid US full-stack engineering job, mid-level to senior, at a stable small to mid-size company.

## Review over skip
- Default to Human review needed, never to Skip. Only Hard Skips end a lead.

## Hard Skips
1. The Never list or a company I ruled out.
2. A firm location rule I cannot meet.
3. Documented maximum base pay clearly below $140,000.
4. Management-only, contract-only or clearance-required roles.
5. A posting confirmed closed.

## Hard requirements (check first)
- Remote or hybrid; United States; senior or mid-level individual contributor.
- Unknowns never disqualify: put them in Human review.

## Compensation
- Floor $140,000 base USD. Posted pay only; estimates never count.

## Level and title
- Software, Senior, Staff or Full-Stack Engineer. Not junior, intern or manager-only.

## Stability
- Prefer profitable or well-funded companies. Note recent layoffs.

## Domains
- Top: developer tools, education. Preferred: health IT, logistics, climate and energy.

## Size
- Prefer 20 to 500 people. Otherwise a note, not a skip.

## Technical fit
- TypeScript, React, Node, PostgreSQL, APIs. Classify gaps as learnable, real or blocking.

## On-call
- Business-hours support is fine. Regular after-hours on-call is a concern: quote it.

## Company vs role decisions
- A bad role does not rule out the company; rule out a company only for a company-wide reason.

## Postings
- Confirm the role is live on the company's own site; flag anything over 45 days.

## Search approach
- Verify the employer and the live role before reporting.

## Reporting filter
- Report only openings worth investigating. Near misses get one line each; size or thin evidence alone is Human review.

## Writing style
Plain, short sentences. Facts exact; "unverified" stays "unverified".

## Output (per lead)
**Company — Position:** company (size, domain, stability); pay as posted; location; pace; culture; tech fit; level; on-call; posting (live yes/no, date); size / stability; concerns; recruiter questions; verdict; link.

## Verdicts
- Strong apply; Worth interviewing; Human review needed; Interesting but concerning; Company worth watching (internal); Skip (only a Hard Skip).

## Cadence and coverage
- Schedules live in `data/schedule.json`. Report coverage honestly.
