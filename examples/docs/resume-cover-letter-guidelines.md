# Resume and cover letter guidelines

Write your rules here. Every kit draft and review follows them. Suggested topics:

- Length: for example, a two-page resume and a one-page cover letter.
- Tone: plain and specific, no filler, no claims you cannot back up.
- Structure: what goes at the top, how many bullets per role, what to cut first.
- Facts: use only what is in `master-resume.md`; never invent employers, dates or numbers.
- Cover letter: open with the point, name one concrete reason for this company, keep it short.
- Gaps: how to frame a missing skill (name it, then point to related work).
